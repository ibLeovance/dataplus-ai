# Matakan Ƙarshe na Ad Networks — Akan Wayarka Kaɗai

Saboda Adsterra da Monetag suna da tsarin CAPTCHA na Cloudflare wanda ke hana shiga daga na'ura ta kai tsaye, duk accounts ɗinka sun riga suna logged-in akan wayarka. Wadannan matakan ƙarshe suna ɗaukar minti 3 kaɗai akan wayarka.

## Mataki 1: Adsterra — Ƙara Website (Mafi Muhimmanci)

Shafin: https://beta.publishers.adsterra.com

1. A dashboard ɗin Adsterra (wurin da aka nuna "ADD WEBSITE"), danna **ADD WEBSITE**.
2. Saka URL: `ai-computer-xplus-ai-fresh.pages.dev`
3. Zaɓi **Category** (zaɓi Technology/Software).
4. Danna **Save/Submit**.
5. Adsterra za ta yi review — yawanci a cikin awa 24 za ta tabbatar (Approved).
6. **Sau biyu:** danna hoton ka ko ka ɗauki screenshot, tura min a nan — zan tabbatar ta shiga Funding Ledger.

## Mataki 2: PropellerAds — Nemo Account ID

Shafin: https://publishers.propellerads.com

1. Danna alamar mutum a sama (menu ɗin ka — kamar yadda ka yi a screenshot).
2. Zaɓi **Account settings**.
3. Nemo lambar **Account ID** (lamba irin ta Adsterra 3429292).
4. Ka tura min lambar a nan ta message.

## Mataki 3: AdSense — Tabbatar da Sites

Shafin: https://adsense.google.com (shiga da pl716290@gmail.com)

1. Danna **Sites** a gefen hagu.
2. Duba ko `ai-computer-xplus-ai-fresh.pages.dev` ko wani site yana ciki.
3. Idan babu, danna **Add site** → saka URL → a bi matakan verification.
4. Publisher ID ɗinka: `pub-7795946429116992` — ya riga yana a Admin Panel.

## Mataki 4: AdMob — Tabbatar

Shafin: https://admob.google.com (shiga da pl716290@gmail.com)

1. Duba dashboard — Publisher ID `pub-2898818886549735` ya riga yana a Admin Panel.

- Saka PropellerAds Account ID a Funding tab.
