## 1️⃣ Abubuwan da aka kammala

### 🔻 Self Deduction (Unlimited)
A cikin kowanne user box na **Notifications Hub** yanzu akwai maballin **Deduct** — zaka iya cire $10, $50, $100, ko duk wani adadi da kake so daga balance na user. Kuma idan ka rage balance ta hanyar **Edit A–Z**, tsarin zai yi deduct atomatik. User zai samu sanarwa nan take.

### ✏️ Edit A–Z (Unlimited) a Hub
Kowanne user box yanzu yana da **Edit A–Z** — zaka iya gyara: username, email, balance, total earned, wallet addresses (BTC/USDT/TRX), da role. Komai da ka save zai shiga account ɗin user kai tsaye.

### 📖 Mark All Reviewed — Sanarwa suna bacewa
A saman Hub akwai sabon maballin **Mark All Reviewed**. Da zarar ka duba sanarwofin ka danna shi, suna **bacewa** — kuma suna sake fitowa kawai idan user ya yi sabon ayyuka. Sabbin ayyuka kuma suna bayyana atomatik tare da alamar ɗaya (badge).

### ✅ Auto Task Review (kowanne user)
A tab **Reviews** akwai **Auto-Approve All Pending** da **Reject All Pending** — danna guda ɗaya zai tabbatar duk submissions na DUKKA users atomatik, kudin zai shiga account nan take, Free Task da VIP Task kuma suna samun sanarwa daban-daban.

### 💸 Withdrawals — Direct Approve
A tab **Withdrawals**, yanzu akwai **Approve / Mark Paid** kai tsaye (TX hash zabi ne kawai) da **Reject** — ba tare da mataki na biyu ba.

### 🚫 Deposits — Invalid Status
A tab **Deposits** yanzu akwai maballai uku: **Approve** (kore), **Reject** (ja), **Invalid** (ja mai duhu — ga receipt ɓarna). Invalid yana tura sanarwa ga user kuma yana bayyana a Hub din admin.

### 📊 Funding da Bots Tabs
Tabs ɗin **Funding** da **Bots** suna nan a Admin Panel azaman shafuka daban (ba a haɗa su da Users ba) — Funding yana da ledger + daily login credit, Bots yana da bots 500 da kudinsu.
